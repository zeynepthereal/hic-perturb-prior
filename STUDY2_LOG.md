## P1.2 Graph-neighbour model (`src/graphnn.py`, `src/test_graphnn.py`) — synthetic data only
- Prediction for a test perturbation = weighted average of the deltas of **training** perturbations whose targets are graph neighbours; weights `S = w0·I + Σ_(h=1..hops) γ^(h-1) P^h`, `P = D⁻¹W` (edge-weighted, k-hop); fallback to the mean training delta if no training perturbation is reachable; targets without a node → NaN row (not predictable, as in the eval_set mechanism). No fitted parameters; defaults `hops=2, γ=0.5, w0=1`. Interface `fit_predict_graph_neighbour(ds, train_idx, test_idx, GraphSource)` mirrors `fit_predict_linear`; `GraphSource.from_hic_graph(name, ds)` reuses the Study 1 bin graphs.
- Tests: planted modular graph with module-specific responses → L2 < 0.45 × mean baseline and correlation with the module signature > 0.95; random graph (5 seeds) → never better than the mean baseline; exact edge-weight arithmetic on a path graph (1 and 2 hops), isolated-node fallback = mean baseline exactly, NaN rows for unmapped targets, same-node weight; dataset interface. **Not run on real data.**

## P1.4 Study 2 revisions: sanity check, third metric, checkpointing + early stopping (no research runs started)
- **Sanity check (set A, target included, top-1000; `scripts/study2_sanity_gears_vs_nochange.py`).** The control mean recomputed GEARS-style from the h5ad equals the dataset control exactly (max abs diff 0.0), gene order identical, mean predicted absolute expression correlates 0.995 with the control. So there is no centring/scaling mismatch. GEARS L2 4.6319 vs no-change 4.6098 is real: GEARS beats no-change in exactly 50% of perturbations. Mean ‖pred Δ‖ 3.78 vs ‖obs Δ‖ 4.61 (ratio 0.82), mean cosine 0.407; the mean predicted delta has norm 3.39 vs 2.14 observed (cosine 0.92), i.e. the shared mean response is over-amplified. Optimal global scale α* = 0.558 gives L2 4.1928; replacing the mean predicted delta by the observed mean gives 4.2702.
- **Third metric** (`src/study2_metrics.py`, tests in `src/test_study2_metrics.py`): Pearson after subtracting, per gene, the mean over the evaluated perturbations from both prediction and observation (constant predictors, e.g. the mean baseline → NaN). Pilot, set A, top-1000, target included: GEARS 0.1926, linear PCA 0.1388 (raw 0.3869 and 0.4007).
- **Checkpointing and early stopping** (`scripts/study2_gears_train.py`, `src/study2_train_utils.py`): per-epoch atomic checkpoint (model, optimizer, scheduler, early stopper, best model, history, RNG states) with automatic resume; max 12 epochs, patience 3; `--monitor mse` (overall val MSE, default) or `mse_de` (top-20 DE MSE, GEARS' own selection). Best-model selection stays GEARS' own (val top-20-DE MSE). The per-epoch full-train-set evaluation that GEARS runs for printing is skipped (it consumes DataLoader RNG, so the data order after epoch 1 differs from GEARS' stream).

## P1.5 Interpretation notes and design decisions (2026-10-29), recorded before the 3 × 3 queue was started
- **Headline (single split, single seed pilot; to be confirmed or refuted by the 3 × 3 design):** the raw metrics are dominated by the shared mean response. Raw Pearson ranks linear PCA slightly above GEARS (0.401 vs 0.387, set A, top-1000, target included); after removing the mean perturbation effect (centred Pearson) the ranking reverses: **GEARS 0.193 vs linear PCA 0.139**. The shuffled-perturbation floors are 0.016 (GEARS) and 0.010 (linear), so both are clearly above floor. Reading: once the shared response is removed, GEARS carries more perturbation-specific signal than the per-gene linear model, while it loses on amplitude calibration (it over-amplifies the shared response: mean-delta amplitude ratio 1.58 vs 1.06 for linear PCA and 0.99 for the mean baseline; per-perturbation norm ratio 0.82 vs 0.53). The finding is "perturbation-specific signal vs amplitude calibration", not "deep model loses". Pilot caveat: one split, one seed, 20 epochs without early stopping.
- **Rescaling is a diagnostic only.** The global factor α* = 0.56 (L2 4.193, below the mean baseline's 4.305) was fitted on the test perturbations; it is **never applied to any reported score**. `scripts/study2_aggregate.py` applies no rescaling.
- **Early stopping aligned on val top-20-DE MSE (`--monitor mse_de`)**, the same metric GEARS uses to pick its best model, so the best epoch is unambiguous. Supersedes the default described in P1.4.
- **Design:** splits `seed1`–`seed3` × training seeds 1–3 (9 GEARS runs, uniform settings, 12 epochs max, patience 3). Mean baseline, no-change and linear PCA are deterministic given a split (3 values each). Reported as median and [min, max] over runs, plus IQR and a between-split vs between-seed decomposition, all three metrics side by side (`results/study2/variance_summary.md`).
- **Scoring additions:** (i) shuffled-perturbation control for GEARS and linear PCA (predictions permuted across the evaluated perturbations, mean of 20 permutations) as the floor for every metric, in particular centred Pearson; linear PCA is the non-constant reference for that column; (ii) amplitude ratios per run: ‖mean predicted Δ‖/‖mean observed Δ‖ and mean ‖predicted Δ‖/mean ‖observed Δ‖ (>1 overshoot). If the overshoot is consistent across splits and seeds it is a property of the model rather than of one run.
- **Deviation recorded:** skipping GEARS' per-epoch train-set evaluation changes the data-loader stream after epoch 1 (DEVIATIONS.md D9); applied to all 9 runs.
- **Queue:** started 2026-10-29 15:51 PDT on AC power (`scripts/study2_run_queue.sh`, resumable). Phase 2, the graph-neighbour model on real data and the control-seed question remain on hold.
